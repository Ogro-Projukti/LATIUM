## 🚀 Welcome to LATIUM

LATIUM is a powerful, all-in-one desktop application designed to streamline your document creation workflow. Whether you're writing academic papers in LaTeX, taking notes in Markdown, or preparing rich PDFs, LATIUM provides a seamless, zero-configuration experience right out of the box.

Forget about complex installations and environment setups. LATIUM is designed for writers, researchers, and creators who want to focus entirely on their content without wrestling with their tools.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Zero Configuration Setup**: No need to manually install LaTeX packages, Python, Node.js, or Git. We bundle everything you need into one simple installer.
- **Integrated PDF Compilation**: Experience fast, built-in PDF compilation directly inside the application, giving you instant previews of your work.
- **Built-in Version Control**: Keep track of your document history safely and effortlessly with an integrated local source control system—no terminal required.
- **Seamless Cloud Integration**: Connect your desktop experience with [LATIUM Cloud](https://latium.cloud) to manage your account, synchronize settings, and unlock advanced features.
- **Modern & Intuitive Interface**: A beautifully crafted editor that stays out of your way, providing a clean and focused space to think and write.

## 📥 Getting Started

Getting started with LATIUM is incredibly easy:

1. Visit our [Releases page](https://github.com/Ogro-Projukti/LATIUM/releases/latest).
2. Download the latest installer for your system (currently available for Windows).
3. Run the setup, launch LATIUM, and start creating!

*Note: Mac and Linux support is coming soon.*
